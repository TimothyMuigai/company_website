# Deeptrack — Full-Site Enterprise Due-Diligence Architecture

## Guiding Principle

Deeptrack’s core offer becomes **enterprise due diligence for identity, media, documents, and AI-generated evidence**. Existing detection products remain valuable, but appear as capability modules inside a larger decision workflow. The site must retain every substantive legacy journey and use the new architecture to make those journeys easier to discover.

## Primary Navigation

| Navigation group | Public destinations | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Due diligence | `/due-diligence`, `/assessment` | Explain the operating model and provide a transparent workflow-exposure intake. |
| Capabilities | `/solution/image-authentication`, `/solution/audio-authentication`, `/solution/gotham`, `/sentinel` | Retain the existing product routes while framing them as evidence, media, and identity capability layers. |
| Industries | `/industries/financial-services`, `/industries/insurance`, `/industries/media`, `/industries/government`, `/industries/flexible-workspaces` | Preserve sector conversion paths under the new enterprise model. |
| Developers | `/productApi`, `/console`, existing external documentation destination | Preserve API, console, and integration workflows. |
| Intelligence | `/research`, `/news`, `/resources/nacha-2026-checklist` | Retain research, gated news-center access, and existing resource content. |
| Partners | `/partners`, `/portal/login` | Add a public partner explanation and retain the existing secure channel-partner portal. |
| Company | `/about`, `/career`, `/contact` | Preserve company, talent, and contact journeys. |
| Investor relations | Existing request-access modals | Keep approved gated investor-material access; do not expose investor materials directly. |

## New Public Destination Routes

| Route | Content role | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| `/assessment` | Reworked calculator as a workflow-exposure intake with transparent inputs and no calculated savings promise. | Do not generate risk scores, ROI, or financial impact claims from user inputs. |
| `/engagements` | Replacement for public price cards, explaining diagnostic, advisory, prototype/build, production hardening, training, and research engagements. | Use “Contact us for a tailored proposal” and “Timeline scoped per engagement.” |
| `/partners` | Channel-partner information page with a route into the existing portal. | Describe program pathways without claiming commission rates, deal volume, certification, or partner logos. |

## Retained Route Reframing

| Existing route or module | Enterprise framing | Retention decision |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage calculator | “Map a workflow exposure” entry point leading to `/assessment`. | Retain and revise. |
| Homepage pricing | “Engagement pathways” entry point leading to `/engagements`. | Retain and revise. |
| Homepage API section | “Build diligence into your workflow” leading to `/productApi`. | Retain and revise. |
| `/productApi` and API pricing | Integration interface for evidence assessment in operational systems. | Retain and revise public commercial language. |
| `/news` and news-center portal | “Due-Diligence Intelligence” and gated public incident/news database. | Retain and reframe entry copy. |
| `/research` and research detail routes | Applied research and evidence-integrity intelligence. | Retain and reframe entry copy. |
| Image/audio/Gotham/Sentinel pages | Evidence-assessment capabilities supporting a diligence record. | Retain and cross-link from capability navigation. |
| Channel Partner Portal | Secure partner operations and materials route. | Retain, with a new public `/partners` explainer. |
| Legacy use-case pages | Existing deep-link assets and search targets. | Retain, then cross-link to new industry pages rather than removing them. |
| Footer and legal destinations | Company, legal, trust, social, and conversion routes. | Restore comprehensively. |

## Footer Architecture

The footer will include a conversion call-to-action followed by eight information groups: **Due Diligence**, **Capabilities**, **Industries**, **Developers**, **Intelligence**, **Partners & Company**, **Legal & Trust**, and **Social**. It must retain the existing Vanta Trust Center, legal center, X, and LinkedIn destinations. Existing public routes will remain available as crawlable deep links instead of being discarded.
